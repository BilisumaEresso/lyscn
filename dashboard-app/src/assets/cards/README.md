Cards are supplied as high-resolution background images.

FORMAT
  JPEG, quality 80–84, sRGB. No text, no QR, no logo baked in.

SIZES
  portrait     1240 x 1748 px   (A6 @ 300 DPI)
  landscape    2480 x 1748 px   (A5 folded table tent @ 300 DPI)
  menu_board   1654 x 2339 px   (A4 @ 200 DPI)

HARD CONSTRAINTS
  1. QR QUIET ZONE — reserve a perfectly flat, single-colour square at least
     1.15x the QR size, with 20% clear padding on all sides. Detail behind a
     QR code breaks scanning.
  2. TEXT SAFE ZONES — any area where restaurant name, tagline, table number
     or payment info sits must be flat or have a built-in scrim/gradient so
     light or dark text holds contrast.
  3. TRIM SAFETY — inset all decorative borders and frames by at least 40px
     so print trimming cannot clip them. Nothing meaningful outside 40px.

FILE SIZE
  Target under 2.5 MB per image after JPEG compression.

DO NOT BAKE IN
  Any restaurant name, logo, table number, payment details or QR code.
  Those are dynamic and drawn at render time.
